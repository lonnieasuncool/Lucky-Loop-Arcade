UNO

Match colors · play wild · say UNO

Free play
Computer

7 cards

Your turn
Your hand

How to play

Play a card matching the discard pile's color or value. Wild cards match anything. If you can't play, draw one card.

Wild cards

Wild changes the color. Wild +4 changes the color and makes the computer draw four.

Local game. No money, accounts, or purchases.
